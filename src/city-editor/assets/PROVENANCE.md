# Historic landmark schematic studies

These three bundled JSON assets are **testable geometric studies**, not reviewed tracings of the source drawings. The SVG and polygons were authored for this repository in local metre coordinates. Source files are retained under `docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/original` and are not included in the app bundle or asset JSON. The floor-plan sources establish the general building type; dimensions come from the references below. Parts without measured coordinates (porticoes, annexes, apses, clearances) are explicitly approximate. Do not present these prototypes as measured restorations.

## Review state

- Source-page licence, bibliographic metadata, digital-file changes and open questions: recorded in [`rights-review.md`](../../../docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/rights-review.md) on 2026-10-01. This is a preliminary rights review, not a distribution opinion for the source images in every jurisdiction.
- Original files: acquired on 2026-10-01 and stored under [`docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/original`](../../../docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/manifest.json), outside the app bundle. The local SHA-1 values match the [Wikimedia Commons Imageinfo API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Imageinfo) for all three files. Local SHA-256, file size, file timestamp, file-page revision ID and timestamp, URL and licence summary are in the manifest. Separate preview renditions remain available for quick visual study.
- A local overlay viewer is generated by `node scripts/review-historic-landmarks.mjs` at [`docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/review.html`](../../../docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/review.html). It reads the original files and plots the prototype footprint, site and entrance with adjustable translation, scale and rotation. Its initial transforms are visual estimates. All three overlays were opened and checked in Chromium on 2026-10-01. Pantheon's round body and portico align in broad proportion; San Vitale's original is horizontal and needs a 90° comparison rotation; Chartres's choir outline was revised after overlay inspection. Exact pixel registration, entrance/annex measurement and construction-period separation: **pending**.
- The same generator writes a directly viewable [Chartres roof study SVG](../../../docs/data/city-landmarks/sources/chartres-roof-study.svg) from the JSON actually embedded by the editor. The user supplied photographs were inspected but are not copied into the asset or repository.
- The user's [1859 section drawing](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/db/86/88/db8688433b661310d28b338bd91bbf79.jpg) and [generic Gothic bay diagram](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/b8/af/59/b8af59a63d6b9d20857fba7818e7fa51.jpg) were reviewed for buttress and roof construction only; neither establishes the eastern roof colour.
- Prototype geometry and offline JSON/SVG loading: covered by `catalog.test.ts`.

The prototypes are named “schematic study” in the editor. On placement, the JSON asset is embedded into the city document, so the resulting map remains available offline.
